Two projects we designed and built

One in London, one in Tallinn. Both are described in full on their own pages.

Case study · London

Lamar Academy

A Mayfair aesthetic clinic and professional training academy needed one website for two very different visitors. We gave patients and practitioners separate routes inside a single brand, with team profiles, treatment pricing and city dates structured so they can be scanned rather than read.

Read the Lamar Academy case study

Case study · Tallinn

Barsion

A managed IT company in Tallinn whose own site was slower than the service it sells. The redesign replaced a repeated card grid with a hairline layout and a single accent colour, and took first contentful paint from 7.7 seconds to under half a second, network requests from 90 to 12, and third-party requests to zero.

Common questions

Do you provide design without development?

Yes. We can deliver design on its own, prepared and documented so your own team can build it.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We redesign existing sites, keeping what works and improving clarity, structure and conversion.

Will the design work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every design is responsive and checked across desktop, tablet and mobile.

Can you use our existing brand identity?

Yes. We design within your brand, and can tighten or extend it where that helps.

Do you create wireframes and prototypes?

Yes. We use wireframes and prototypes to agree structure and flow before visual design.

Can ICE WIND also develop the finished website?

Yes. We can design and build the same project, which removes the hand-off between separate design and development agencies.
